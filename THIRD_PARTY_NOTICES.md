## Open English WordNet

Part of the Across crossword answer-and-clue corpus is adapted from **Open
English WordNet 2025**, maintained by the Open English WordNet Community:
<https://en-word.net/>.

Open English WordNet is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

The generated Across subset normalizes answer spelling, selects one definition
per answer, assigns a puzzle category and difficulty, filters unsuitable
entries, and omits entries already present in the original Across corpus.

Open English WordNet is derived from Princeton WordNet. The Princeton WordNet
license and attribution information are available at:
<https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use>.
